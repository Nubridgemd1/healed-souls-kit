Mental Health Associated with Widowhood

This page shows how the flip-book reader is embedded on the Healed Souls website. Readers get a free preview; after they pay, the site tells the reader to unlock — no page reload needed.

How to integrate on hsphcs.com

Drop the reader in an <iframe>. When your checkout (Paystack / Stripe) confirms payment, unlock it one of two ways:

<!-- 1) Embed the reader -->
<iframe id="reader" src="widowhood-ebook.html"></iframe>

<!-- 2a) After a successful payment, tell the reader to unlock: -->
<script>
  document.getElementById('reader').contentWindow.postMessage(
    { type: 'hs-unlock', token: 'the-buyer-order-id' }, '*'
  );
</script>

<!-- 2b) …or, for a returning buyer, load it already unlocked: -->
<iframe src="widowhood-ebook.html?unlocked=1"></iframe>

The reader also emits {type:'hs-checkout-request'} to the parent window when a reader taps Pay & read now, so you can open your checkout modal. Manual sales can use the access code set in the reader’s CFG.accessCode. Unlock persists on the device via localStorage.
